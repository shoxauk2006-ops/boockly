## Short description

Skedwoo lets customers choose a service, specialist, date and available time through Telegram. Businesses manage bookings, services, specialists, schedules and time blocks from one simple workspace.

## Medium description

Skedwoo is a Telegram-first online booking system for appointment-based businesses. Customers can choose a service, specialist, date and available time without calling or waiting for a reply. Business owners manage services, staff, working hours, blocked times and bookings from a simple Telegram workflow. Skedwoo works well for barbers, tutors, coaches, massage therapists and other small service businesses.

## Long description

Skedwoo helps service businesses replace repetitive appointment messages with a clear self-service booking flow.

The business sets up its services, specialists, prices, duration, working hours and unavailable periods. It then shares a personal booking link or QR code. Customers open the booking page in Telegram, choose a service and specialist, see the available schedule and confirm a suitable time.

New appointments stay organized in one workspace, and booking notifications can be received through Telegram. A single Skedwoo account can also manage multiple businesses with separate services, specialists and schedules.

Skedwoo is designed for small appointment-based businesses such as barbers, private tutors, coaches and massage therapists. Monthly plans include a one-time seven-day trial.
