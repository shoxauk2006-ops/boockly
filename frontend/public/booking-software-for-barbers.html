A simple alternative to booking by chat.

Manual appointment messages work when there are only a few clients, but they become difficult to track as the business grows. The same time can be discussed with two people, messages can be missed and clients have to wait for a reply. A booking page gives them immediate access to the available schedule.

Skedwoo keeps the setup focused on what a small barbershop needs: services, specialists, working hours, time blocks, booking notifications and a client link. It does not require clients to install a separate business app because the booking flow opens through Telegram.
