Rules of Use

These rules are intended to keep Bookly useful, safe and fair for businesses and their customers.

Effective: September 15, 2026

1. Use Bookly for legitimate business activity

Bookly may only be used for lawful services and legitimate business purposes. Do not use the platform to facilitate fraud, scams, illegal activity or services that are prohibited by applicable law.

2. Keep business information accurate

Business owners are responsible for keeping their business name, services, specialists, schedules and booking information accurate and up to date.

3. Do not mislead customers

Do not publish false, deceptive or materially incomplete information about your business, services, availability, prices or identity.

4. Respect customer information

Only collect and use customer information that is reasonably necessary for providing and managing bookings. Handle customer data responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

5. No spam or abusive use

Do not use Bookly or its Telegram integrations to send spam, harassment, threats, malicious content or unsolicited messages.

6. Bookings and cancellations

Businesses are responsible for honoring the availability they publish and for communicating any changes or cancellations to customers. Customers are expected to provide accurate booking information and respect the business's stated booking and cancellation conditions.

7. Account security

Keep your Bookly and connected Telegram account secure. Do not share passwords, authentication codes or other credentials. You are responsible for activity performed through your account.

8. Service availability

Bookly provides technical tools for online booking and business management. Bookly does not guarantee the quality, safety or legality of services offered by businesses using the platform.

9. Violations

We may restrict, suspend or terminate access when necessary to protect Bookly, its users or third parties, or when an account is used in violation of these rules or applicable law.

10. Support

For questions about these rules, your account or Bookly, contact the support team.
