FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do customers book?

Customers open your Bookly booking page in Telegram, choose a service, specialist, date and available time.

Do I need a separate website?

No. Your booking flow can work through Telegram.

Do I receive booking notifications?

Yes. New bookings can be sent to you directly in Telegram.

Can I manage multiple businesses?

Yes. One Bookly account can contain multiple businesses.

Can I change my service limit later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your service package from Billing.

Is there a free trial?

Monthly plans include a one-time 7-day trial per Bookly account. Annual plans do not include a trial.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Subscription cancellation is handled from your account billing settings.
