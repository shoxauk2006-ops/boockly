FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Bookly?

Bookly is an online booking system that gives you one workspace for services, availability and appointments, plus a customer-facing booking flow.

Do my customers need an account?

No. The booking experience is designed to be simple and can be used without forcing customers through a separate account flow.

Can I manage Bookly from Telegram?

Yes. After connecting your Telegram account, your existing Bookly business can be managed through the Bookly Mini App.

How does billing work?

Bookly subscriptions are handled on the Bookly website. You can choose the available monthly or yearly plan there.
