Useful for independent tutors and small teaching teams.

An independent tutor can use Skedwoo as a simple availability page, while a small tutoring business can add multiple specialists and assign services to the right people. Each lesson can have its own duration and price, and the schedule determines which times the student can select.

This reduces repeated scheduling messages without removing personal communication. You can still talk to students in Telegram, but routine time selection happens through a structured booking flow. Monthly plans include a one-time seven-day trial, allowing you to test the complete setup with your own schedule.
