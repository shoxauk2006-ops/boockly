Why use Telegram for appointments?

Many small service businesses already talk to clients in Telegram. Moving the booking flow into the same environment removes the need for a separate customer app and reduces repeated questions such as “What times are available?” The customer can see open slots directly, while the owner keeps the business profile, services, team and schedule organized.

Skedwoo is designed for barbers, tutors, coaches, massage therapists and other appointment-based businesses. Monthly plans include a one-time seven-day trial, so a business can prepare its booking flow before deciding whether to continue.
